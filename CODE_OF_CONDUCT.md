# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

Examples of expected behavior include being considerate, communicating constructively, accepting good-faith feedback, and focusing criticism on ideas rather than people. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, doxxing, and sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.
